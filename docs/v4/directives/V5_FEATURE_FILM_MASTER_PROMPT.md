- Walking style and body language.
- Acting characteristics.
- Wardrobe library.

Maintain facial identity, body proportions and voice identity throughout every shot.

Implement automatic identity-drift detection, reference-based generation and selective regeneration.

Never silently substitute an established actor with a newly generated face.
